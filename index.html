OUR CELEBRATION

More than a festival.
A community.

Seattle Diwali brings people together through light, color, music and traditions of Diwali.

Our 2026 celebration is designed as a welcoming experience for families, friends, performers, local businesses, community organizations and everyone who wants to celebrate together.

THE VENUE

Rosehill Community Center.

Perched above Puget Sound in Mukilteo, Rosehill's wood-and-glass great room and waterside patio sets the stage for this year's celebration.
