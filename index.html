OUR CELEBRATION

More than a festival.
A community.

Seattle Diwali brings people together through the light, color, music and traditions of Diwali.

Our 2026 celebration is designed as a welcoming experience for families, friends, performers, local businesses, community organizations and everyone who wants to celebrate together.
